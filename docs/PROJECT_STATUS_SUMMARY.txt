11. BUSINESS LOGIC
================================================================================

Revenue ≠ Cash Recognition:
  - System separates Revenue (when invoice issued) from Cash (when paid)
  - AR Aging shows how much cash is "blocked" in receivables
  - Expected Collection Schedule (Phase 2) will show cash inflow timing

Direct Method Formula:
  Cash from Customers = Sales + Opening AR - Closing AR
  (Adjusts for AR change to convert revenue to cash received)

Indirect Method Formula:
  Operating Cash Flow = Net Profit
                       + Depreciation & Amortization
                       - Bad Debt & Allowance
                       - FX Loss / + FX Gain
                       - Interest Paid (if not in P&L)
                       + AR Decrease / - AR Increase
                       + Inventory Decrease / - Inventory Increase
                       - AP Decrease / + AP Increase
                       - Accruals Decrease / + Accruals Increase

Cash Flow Activities (TAS 7):
  Operating: Day-to-day business activities
  Investing: Purchase/sale of fixed assets
  Financing: Borrowing/repayment of loans

13. KNOWN LIMITATIONS & ASSUMPTIONS
================================================================================

Mock Data:
  - All data is simulated; not connected to actual systems
  - 6 months of history (Oct 2024 - Mar 2026)
  - Realistic amounts & business patterns but not actual company data

No Authentication:
  - No login/user authentication (assume all users trusted)
  - No role-based access control (all users see all data)

No Persistence:
  - Data is not saved (page refresh resets any manual changes)
  - Suitable for demo/presentation, not production use

Browser Compatibility:
  - Requires modern browser (Chrome 90+, Safari 14+, Firefox 88+)
  - Uses React 18, ES6, CSS Grid
  - Not tested on IE11

Performance:
  - Bundle size: 339 KB (acceptable for most connections)
  - Load time: ~1-2 seconds (on typical internet connection)
  - No pagination (all data loaded at once)
